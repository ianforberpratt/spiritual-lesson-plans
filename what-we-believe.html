What We Believe

Where these lessons come from

This isn't an institution and there's nothing to join. Here's a plain-language look at the ideas underneath every lesson on this site.

In short

What is Christian Science?

Christian Science is a Christian denomination founded in the 1870s by Mary Baker Eddy, grounded in the Bible and her book Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures. Its central idea is simple to say and takes a lifetime to live: God is entirely good, and everything God creates — including you — reflects that goodness completely.

In practice, that means Christian Science teaches that your true identity isn't fragile, flawed, or something you have to earn. You are already whole, spiritually complete, and loved without condition, right now, not as a reward for good behavior or right belief.

That single idea is why it sits underneath a resource for mentoring young people: it's a starting point that asks nothing of anyone before they belong.

How it relates to Christianity

Same roots, a different lens

Christian Science is a branch of Christianity — it centers on Jesus' teaching and healing work, and treats the Bible as its foundation. Where it differs from many other Christian traditions is in emphasis: it reads Jesus' healings as demonstrations of spiritual laws anyone can learn to apply, not as one-time miracles reserved for him alone.

It also leans harder into the idea that spiritual reality — who you are in God's eyes — is more fundamentally true than material circumstances like illness, fear, or failure. Other Christian traditions describe that same hope in different language and with different practices. This site draws on the Christian Science framing because it's the one its author knows best, not because it claims to be the only valid one.

The ideas behind every lesson

A few threads you'll see again and again

01

You are already whole

Not broken and in need of fixing. Not incomplete until you achieve something. Whole now, as a starting point — not a finish line.

02

Love isn't conditional

God's love, as Christian Science describes it, doesn't fluctuate with behavior, doubt, or belief. That's the model every lesson tries to offer a young person, too.

03

Prayer is a way of thinking, not asking

Rather than a request sent to a distant God, prayer here is treated as a way of aligning your own thought with what's already true — more listening than asking.

Every faith background welcome

You don't have to believe any of this to use these lessons

Whatever tradition you or the young people you mentor come from, these ideas are offered as a lens, not a requirement. Take what's useful, leave what isn't, and adapt freely.
