What We Believe

Where these lessons come from

This isn't an institution and there's nothing to join. Here's a plain-language look at the ideas underneath every lesson on this site.

Our core approach

A few threads you'll see again and again

01

You are already whole

Not broken and in need of fixing. Not incomplete until you achieve something. Whole now, as a starting point — not a finish line.

02

Love isn't conditional

God's love, as Christian Science describes it, doesn't fluctuate with behavior, doubt, or belief. That's the model every lesson tries to offer a young person, too.

03

Prayer is a way of thinking, not asking

Rather than a request sent to a distant God, prayer here is treated as a way of aligning your own thought with what's already true — more listening than asking.

Every faith background welcome

You don't have to believe any of this to use these lessons

Whatever tradition you or the young people you mentor come from, these ideas are offered as a lens, not a requirement. Take what's useful, leave what isn't, and adapt freely.
