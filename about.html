About

Why this exists

No organization, no board, no budget. Just a resource, built to be given away.

The short version

Built with the belief that every young person deserves to hear this

Spiritual Lesson Plans started as a simple conviction: mentors, teachers, supporters of high schoolers often just need a prompt or a starter-pack of ideas to have conversations about faith, identity, and belonging. These materials shouldn't require a curriculum budget, a denominational badge, or a background in theology. They just require someone willing to sit down and say, plainly, you are already whole — and mean it.

This site is that conversation, written down and made shareable. It draws on Christian Science teaching, adult-learning pedagogy, and research on how younger generations actually want to be taught — not lectured at, not tested, just met where they are.

What this is not

No strings attached

  • Not a nonprofit, church, or organization — there's no entity behind this site to join, fund, or report to.
  • No donation ask, anywhere, ever.
  • No account required to read, use, print, or adapt a lesson.
  • No data collected about you or the young people you mentor.

Free to share

Take it, adapt it, pass it on

Every lesson here is released under Creative Commons — use it in a youth group, a classroom, a paid program, wherever it's needed. Just credit the source and share your version alike.
