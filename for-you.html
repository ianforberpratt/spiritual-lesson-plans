For you

You don't need an adult in the room for this part.

Everything else on this site is built for whoever's leading you through something — a parent, a teacher, a youth leader. This page isn't. Use it by yourself, send it to a friend, or bring it to whoever's already walking with you. Your call.

Three ways to use this

However you got here, there's a way in

01

On your own

Nobody has to lead this for it to count. Try the three-minute experience below, or tap what's going on beneath it and read a lesson yourself — no group, no adult, no permission needed.

02

With a friend

If something here actually lands, send it. Go through it separately and talk after, or sit down together and do it side by side — either way works.

03

Hand it up

If something here opens up more than a few minutes can hold, that's not a sign to stop — it's a sign to bring in someone who can sit with it longer. Hand this to a parent, teacher, or youth leader; For Mentors tells them exactly how to lead it, no training required.
