# Brief: Five Candidate Lesson Topics

Written 2026-08-28, from a gap analysis of the 18 lessons live at the time. For Ian to hand to
his authoring Project — framing and scope suggestions only, no lesson content drafted here.
Each one flags what it must NOT overlap with, since a couple sit close to existing lessons.

## 1. Anxiety about what's coming
**The gap:** everything adjacent (`off-days-are-allowed`, `trust-the-mover`) processes something
that already happened. Nothing addresses the future-facing kind — dread, a spiraling "what if,"
the stomach-knot before something hasn't happened yet.
**Keep distinct from:** `off-days-are-allowed` (present low mood), `trust-the-mover`
(already-happened disappointment).
**Likely bands:** all five — anxiety shows up at every age, just differently expressed.
**Topic tag:** probably `disappointment-and-hard-days`, though it may earn its own category once
written, the way `grief-and-loss` did — worth revisiting after a draft exists.

## 2. When a friendship just ends
**The gap:** no fight, no villain, no dramatic falling-out — a close friendship just fades, and
there's rarely language for that being its own real loss.
**Keep distinct from:** `when-someone-feels-like-the-enemy` (assumes active tension),
`lonely-in-a-crowded-room` (the general ache, not tied to one specific relationship). **Also
distinct from `grief-doesnt-need-fixing`**, which already uses "a friend group breaking apart" as
an 8-11 example — a new lesson should focus on one central friendship, the no-drama drift version,
and probably skew older (11-14+) where the grief lesson's own examples have already moved on to
death/major loss.
**Likely bands:** 8-11 through 21-plus; 5-8 may be too abstract for "drifting apart" as opposed to
a concrete fight.
**Topic tag:** genuinely ambiguous between `belonging-and-loneliness` and `grief-and-loss` — an
open question, not a call to make in advance.

## 3. Body image
**The gap:** touched in one line (`soul-another-name-for-god`'s 11-14 band: "a hard feeling about
your body") but never its own lesson, despite how load-bearing this is at 11-14 and 14-21.
**Keep distinct from:** `not-the-struggle` (general emotional discomfort, not body-specific).
**Likely bands:** 8-11 through 21-plus; 5-8 is a judgment call.
**Flag:** this will likely need `sensitive_topic: requires-careful-adaptation` (safeguarding
notes), same pattern as the grief lesson — not `teen-adult-only`, since it's appropriate across
most ages, just needs facilitator care.
**Topic tag:** `identity-and-worth`.

## 4. Social media and comparison culture
**The gap:** `big-ego-little-ego`'s 14-21 band gestures at "the most little-e-ego-saturated
environment ever built," but nothing sits squarely on the curated-feed, everyone-else's-highlight-
reel mechanism specifically — which works differently than general pride/ego or the
performance-scorekeeping in `is-god-keeping-score`.
**Keep distinct from:** `big-ego-little-ego` (general ego mechanism), `is-god-keeping-score`
(performance/moral scorekeeping, not social comparison).
**Likely bands:** 11-14 through 21-plus; younger bands probably don't have enough social media
exposure yet to anchor the lesson in lived experience — author's call.
**Topic tag:** `identity-and-worth`.

## 5. "Do I even believe this anymore?"
**The gap:** `doubt-and-hard-questions-about-god` is specific theological confusion (is God
keeping score, what does a word mean). Nothing addresses the bigger identity-level question older
teens and young adults actually carry — not "what does this mean" but "am I still someone who
believes this at all."
**Keep distinct from:** `doubt-and-hard-questions-about-god`'s existing lessons (`words-that-open-
doors`, `seven-synonyms-and-three-degrees`, `is-god-keeping-score`) — different weight, an
identity crisis rather than a definitional one.
**Likely bands:** 14-21 and 21-plus only — this is not a 5-8 or 8-11 concern.
**Topic tag:** `doubt-and-hard-questions-about-god`, though it may also warrant its own
distinction eventually.
