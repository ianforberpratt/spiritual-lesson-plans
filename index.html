Why this exists

Young people can tell when they're being talked down to.

So these lessons don't. They take a teenager's questions seriously, give them something real to do, and never make belief the price of admission.

01 — Grounded

Rooted, not required

Written with inspiration from the Christian Science faith teachings and open to any faith background, including none. Every lesson starts from the same place: no one needs fixing to be worthy of love.

02 — Interactive

Something to do, not sit through

Built on adult-learning pedagogy and current research on how young people actually engage. Every lesson alternates a little input, a little doing, a little reflecting.

03 — Honest

Room for the hard parts

Doubt, off days, real disappointment and anger are treated as normal, not as problems to fix. Each lesson tells you what to do when it isn't going well.

For mentors

You don't have to be a theologian. You just have to show up.

Every lesson is written to be picked up and led the same week — by a parent, a teacher, a youth leader, an older sibling. No training, no curriculum to buy, no prep beyond reading it once.

Each one tells you how long it takes, what the room needs to feel like, what to do if a young person shuts down, and when a conversation has moved past what a lesson plan should handle.

How to lead one

What's in every lesson

  • A time estimate you can trust — and permission to use only part of it.
  • Learning goals written as what a young person walks away with, not what you covered.
  • A beat-by-beat flow with timings, discussion prompts, and printable handouts.
  • Tiered “if it's not going well” guidance, including what to do if something serious surfaces.
