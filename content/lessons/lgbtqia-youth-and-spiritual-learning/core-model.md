# Lesson Core Model: "LGBTQIA+ Youth and Spiritual Learning"

*Shared scaffolding — never taught directly to any age group.*

## Why only one band exists

No 5-8, 8-11, or 11-14 version of this lesson has been built, deliberately — not an oversight. This
is the site's "fit what naturally works, don't force it" principle applied to genuinely high-stakes
content: this lesson's own safety section names family-rejection and self-harm risk as the room it's
actually holding, and identity-disclosure framing at that level isn't appropriate to introduce to
elementary or early-middle-school children in a Sunday School setting without family and professional
context this site can't provide. If younger-age belonging/anti-bullying content is wanted (e.g.,
"everyone is loved by God no matter what anyone else says about them"), that's better built as its
own general lesson than as a downward adaptation of this one.

No 21+ version exists either — this lesson is squarely youth-identity-formation focused. An
adult-facing companion (for parents or mentors processing their own reaction) would be a different
lesson, and likely belongs on the For Mentors page rather than in the age-band lesson library.
