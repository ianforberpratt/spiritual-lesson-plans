title: Semper Paratus
framing: Readiness was never about outrunning the next disaster. It's a description of who's already got you.
quote: Divinity is always ready. Semper paratus is Truth's motto.
citation: Science and Health, Mary Baker Eddy, p. 458:14-15
body: Three numbered steps, each with a short label and one-line explanation. 1 — Feel it honestly: name the actual fear, out loud, to someone or to God (Isaiah 43:1, 4, NIV). 2 — Choose which fire to feed: bring the fear into prayer instead of outrage; master the propensity, not the person (Science and Health, p. 405:5-9). 3 — Radiate a ready posture: stay rooted enough in wholeness to actually help when it's your turn (Science and Health, p. 458:14-15).
pull_line: Preparation isn't stockpiling. It's staying so rooted in wholeness that when the moment comes, you're free to actually help.
