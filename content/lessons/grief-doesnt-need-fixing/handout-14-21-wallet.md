title: Neither Kind Is Worse
framing: A goodbye you saw coming and one that blindsided you are different shapes of the same real loss — and adjusting to either one is real work, not a mood with a deadline.
quote: Divine Love always has met and always will meet every human need.
citation: Science and Health, Mary Baker Eddy, p. 494:10
pull_line: You don't have to rank your grief against anyone else's — or finish adjusting on schedule — to have it count.
three_questions:
  1. Is the loss you're carrying one you saw coming, or one that surprised you — or both?
  2. What's one real strength, person, or practice you actually have right now?
  3. What would it look like to let that be true on a day when it doesn't feel like enough?
