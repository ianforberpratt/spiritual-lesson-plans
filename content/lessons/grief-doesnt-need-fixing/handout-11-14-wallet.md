title: The Map, Not the Schedule
framing: Even the doctor who named the "five stages of grief" said they don't happen in order — this is a map of common ground, not a checklist with a deadline.
quote: Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God.
citation: Isaiah 40:1, KJV
pull_line: You don't have to be in the "right" stage — or be done adjusting — to be comforted.
three_questions:
  1. Which feeling on the map has shown up for you lately, in any order?
  2. Was this loss one you saw coming, or one that surprised you?
  3. What's one thing (or person) that's actually helping right now?
