title: The Loop
framing: Even the doctor who named the five stages of grief said they don't happen in a straight line — here's what the real shape looks like, and why it takes as long as it takes.
quote: Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God.
citation: Isaiah 40:1, KJV
body: A circular diagram with five labeled points around a loop — Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance — connected by curved arrows going every direction between every point, captioned "You can start anywhere, skip any point, repeat any point, or feel two at once — for as long as it takes." A small side note distinguishes, without ranking, "A goodbye you saw coming" and "A goodbye that surprised you" — both feeding into the same loop.
pull_line: You don't have to be in the "right" point on the loop — or finished with it — to be comforted.
reflection_prompts:
  - Which point(s) on the loop have shown up for you lately, in any order?
  - Was your loss one you saw coming, or one that surprised you, or both?
