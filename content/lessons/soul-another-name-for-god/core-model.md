# Lesson Core Model: "Soul: Another Name for God"

*Shared scaffolding for "Soul: Another Name for God" — never taught directly to any age group. Read
this before writing or teaching any band's version. Per BUILD-BRIEF.md section 6.3, the existing
content (originally written for "High school 14-18") maps cleanly onto the new 14-21 band as-is —
just retag it. Two genuine gaps are filled here: an 11-14 version and a 21+ version. This topic is
not appropriate for 5-8 or 8-11 in any form — see "Why only three bands" below.*

## The spiritual core (constant across all ages)

**Central claim:** Soul is one of the seven names for God, and it is the one true, permanent source
of a person's identity, sense, and selfhood — not a possession, not a feeling, not something that
can be damaged or lost. The body and its impulses are meant to express Soul, not define it. When a
person locates their real identity in Soul instead of in a propensity or an impulse, that propensity
loses its authority over them.

**The picture being replaced:** identity as something that comes from the body — its appetites, its
impulses, its reputation, its reflection in a screen — so that a bad impulse or a bad moment feels
like proof of who someone really is underneath.

**The picture being offered:** Soul as the real, unchanging source of identity underneath any single
impulse or mistake — meaning a propensity (hatred, lust, revenge, deceit) is something a person can
notice and outweigh, not something that defines them.

**Anchor citations (used at whatever depth fits the age band):**
- "Christian Science commands man to master the propensities, — to hold hatred in abeyance with
  kindness, to conquer lust with chastity, revenge with charity, and to overcome deceit with
  honesty. Choke these errors in their early stages, if you would not cherish an army of conspirators
  against health, happiness, and success." — Science and Health, Mary Baker Eddy, p. 405:5–11
- "The good man finally can overcome his fear of sin. This is sin's necessity, — to destroy itself.
  Immortal man demonstrates the government of God, good, in which is no power to sin." — Science and
  Health, p. 405:18

## Learning objective family

1. **Notice** that an impulse (hatred, lust, revenge, deceit, or their age-appropriate equivalents)
   is a passing "conspirator," not proof of who they really are.
2. **Feel**, not just understand intellectually, that a real, permanent identity exists underneath
   any single bad moment.
3. **Carry** one of the four outweighing qualities — kindness, chastity/self-respect, charity,
   honesty — into a real moment this week.
4. **Locate** one real, current situation where they can practice answering from that deeper
   identity instead of from impulse or reputation.

## Why only three bands, not five

Per the site's "fit what naturally works, don't force it" principle (BUILD-BRIEF.md section 6.3):
this lesson names real propensities directly, including sexual and relational material that isn't
appropriate to introduce, even in translated form, below early adolescence. There is no 5-8 or 8-11
version of this lesson, by design, not by oversight — a younger child's sense of identity and impulse
control is genuinely different territory, already served by other lessons on this site (like "Is God
Keeping Score?").

## The bridge metaphor, by age band

The four propensities stay constant; what changes is which real, current version of each one gets
named.

| Age band | Hatred → kindness | Lust → chastity | Revenge → charity | Deceit → honesty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11-14 | Pile-on comments, group exclusion | Reframed toward body image and appearance pressure — comparison, not sexual content | Subtweeting, freezing a friend out, petty payback | A curated identity online that doesn't match real life; lying to fit in |
| 14-21 (existing, as-is) | Pile-on comments under a post | Pressure around hookup culture, how a body "should" look online | Subtweeting, screenshotting to expose someone, freezing a friend out | A curated online identity that doesn't match anyone's actual life |
| 21+ | Online pile-ons, professional rivalry, political contempt | Infidelity, objectification, mishandled workplace attraction | Petty workplace retaliation, gossip, the silent treatment in a marriage | Professional dishonesty, a curated adult social presence, dishonesty in a relationship |

## What does NOT get inherited across age bands

- **Sexual specificity.** "Lust" is reframed toward body image and comparison at 11-14, not
  translated literally. Only 14-21 and 21+ use it in anything close to its ordinary sense, and even
  then, at the level of pattern (pressure, comparison, curated image), not explicit content.
- **Workplace and marriage material.** Stays in the 21+ version only, the same rule already
  established for "Is God Keeping Score?"
- **Facilitator safeguarding intensity.** The 11-14 version needs the strongest safeguarding notes
  of the three, given body image is real developmental-risk territory at that age.

## Facilitator-coaching principle

At every band, keep the four propensities at the level of pattern and story, not personal
confession. If a participant volunteers something real and specific, follow their lead — never chase
it, and never treat this as a behavior-correction or confession exercise.
