eyebrow: A VISUAL · WHAT ACTUALLY LASTS
title: The Ice Sculpture and the Spring
framing: One is the most impressive thing in the room, melting from the moment it's finished. The other is the least impressive thing on the property, and has never once run dry.
quote: The immortal, spiritual man is really substantial, and reflects the eternal substance... which mortals hope for.
citation: Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures, Mary Baker Eddy, p. 301:6–12
body: |
  Two columns side by side, headed "Ice Sculpture (Feels Solid)" and "Spring (Actually Lasts),"
  each with a simple icon (a carved ice sculpture under a spotlight; a small natural spring).

  Paired lines, one row per pair, under the two headers:
  - Ice Sculpture: The center of attention right now / Spring: Easy to walk right past
  - Ice Sculpture: Took a lot of effort to look this impressive / Spring: Never had to perform
  - Ice Sculpture: Melting from the moment it's finished / Spring: Still running long after the
    party's over
  - Ice Sculpture: money, looks, being right, followers, a grade / Spring: honesty, understanding,
    love, courage
pull_line: Look for what's still standing quietly — not what's shining the loudest.
reflection_prompts:
  - One thing in my life right now that feels like the biggest deal in the world:
  - Is it the ice-sculpture kind, or the spring kind — and how do I know?
