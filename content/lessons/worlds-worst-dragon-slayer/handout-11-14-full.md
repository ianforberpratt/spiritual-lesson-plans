body: |
  One large, simple dragon outline fills most of the page: seven heads, ten horns, drawn friendly-dramatic rather than frightening. Around the outside of the dragon, four short callouts in a loud, bold style, labeled "THE COSTUME": HUGE · LOUD · EVERYWHERE · RIGHT NOW.

  In the dragon's belly, a small, plain circle labeled "INSIDE THE COSTUME," with blank space to write or draw in, the label "What I'm actually afraid of losing:", and a small line beneath it: "Sized right, it's something you can finally do something about."

  A soft golden beam of light enters from one corner, labeled "TRUTH + LOVE," with this caption on the beam: "Truth and Love prevail against the dragon because the dragon cannot war with them." (Science and Health, p. 567:10-12)

  Below the dragon, two side-by-side boxes:
  - TRUTH: Not "well, actually." Standing steady and asking, "What specifically am I afraid of losing?" until the dragon is its real size.
  - LOVE: Not just a feeling. One true, kind, practical thing you can do today: a text, a bridge, a hand.

  A slim line at the very bottom, in small italic: "A person is never the dragon. The fear, the lie, and the hate wrapped around a problem are."
