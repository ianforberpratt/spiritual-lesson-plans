title: Permission Comes First
framing: We tend to think permission has to be earned — that we get to rest, or feel loved, or feel whole, once the hard part is behind us. The story runs the other direction: the permission comes first, in the water, in the not-yet.
quote: Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness.
citation: Matthew 3:15, KJV
body: A paired list, headed "Waiting to Earn It" and "Already True Now" — Rest, once the deadline's behind me / Permitted to rest in the middle of it; Feeling loved, once I'm through this hard stretch / Named beloved before any of the work starts; Feeling whole, once the fog clears / Already immersed in a Spirit that's already here; A better version of me, later / This version, now — not forever, not without hope of change, just permitted for today. Below the list, a short pull-quote set apart: "Baptism. Purification by Spirit; submergence in Spirit." — Science and Health, Mary Baker Eddy, p. 581.
pull_line: Love was never waiting for the fixing to be finished before it showed up.

  - One real, current thing I'm waiting to fix before I'll let myself feel okay:
