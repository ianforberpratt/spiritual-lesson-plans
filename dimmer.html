A three-minute experience

Dimmer

Not anxious. Not depressed. Just… dim. Take three minutes.

Have you felt dull lately? Just not yourself? It isn’t one of the feelings people talk about, so it rarely gets noticed, even by you.

Dimmer helps you think it through, and gives you a tool you can use right now. In three minutes you get a card of “Your Dimmer Settings”: how you go dim, what turns you up, a one-minute tool for right now, one small thing to try this week, and a line to hold onto. It is a mirror, not a diagnosis.

It is open to any tradition and not tied to any one faith. It is free, nothing you type is saved or tracked, and you can stop any time.

This experience needs JavaScript to run. Nothing is saved or sent either way. If it does not start, try reloading, or open this page in another browser.

If this feels like more than numb, please talk to someone. In the US, you can call or text 988. Elsewhere, search for your local crisis line.
