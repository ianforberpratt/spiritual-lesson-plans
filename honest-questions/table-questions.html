Table Questions

Three questions for any church meeting or group discussion.

1

Honor

What do we love about how we gather, and want to honor and carry into the future?

2

Welcome

Who isn’t here yet, or used to be, and what might they need from us?

3

Build

What’s one small thing we could build together before we meet again?

At the table

  • Listen without fixing.
  • Speak for yourself.
  • No judging, including ourselves.
  • No one has to speak.
  • Not a change to the service, but a conversation around it.

Take five or ten minutes for each question, in pairs first if the group is large. The point is for everyone to feel seen, heard, and part of the conversation.
