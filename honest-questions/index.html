Why I Keep Writing to You (and What’s Coming)

You’ve been hearing from me a lot lately, and you deserve to know why.

I teach a Sunday school class. Every week I watch teenagers who are hungry for meaning and wholeness, and many of them feel unheard. I believe Christian Science has what they’re looking for, and that it doesn’t always reach them in a form they can engage with. I can’t stay quiet about that. This isn’t because I’m special. I’ve been given a great deal, and keeping silent would feel dishonest.

So over the next ten days, I’ll share three short posts about two things we often hesitate to say out loud: how we talk about our shrinking numbers, and how we show human compassion. I write as a fellow traveler. I feel this too.

One post every three days at 6 PM Central, and everything will gather in one place so you can catch up or read it all together.

For the skeptics among us: no platitudes. We’ll examine the two fears through three lenses: the gap between what we say and what we do, the commitments we hold without naming them, and grief. Each comes with claims you can test against your own experience.

Where I stand

I’m not a critic or a detractor. I love Christian Science. I’m a member of the Mother Church and class taught, and what I write is rooted in the Bible and Science and Health, and I aim to stay grounded in the Manual. I won’t get it perfect. I’m a learner, and I welcome being shown where I’ve missed. I don’t speak for the Church. I’m one loving Christian Scientist, following what I feel led to do. Honest questions, asked in love, move us forward. Uncomfortable doesn’t mean unloving.

I’d love your thoughts, whatever they are. I’m glad you’re here.
