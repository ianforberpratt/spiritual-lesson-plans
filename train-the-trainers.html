A hands-on workshop

How do we share Christian Science, and how does it land?

A hands-on workshop for Christian Scientists who want to talk about Christian Science in words that meet the listener's current needs. Nothing about the truth of being changes. We're only looking at the door we hold open.

Who it's for

For Christian Scientists who want to share its ideas, its tools, and its Science in words today's world can hear. By invitation, as a train-the-trainers event.

Where it comes from

Built from the Honest Questions CS series: honest questions, asked in love, about how we share what we love.

How it works

This is a train-the-trainers event, by invitation. You begin by feeling the door held open for you. Then you learn the model, take it home, and run it with others. Each replication reports back, including anonymous feedback from the people you shared with. You're measured on fidelity to the spirit of the model, and on whether listeners feel cared for right where they are.

The heart of the method

The workshop rests on three things, built on adult learning principles.

  1. Reflective competence

    You begin by being cared for, and by seeing your strengths in how you already share your practice. Then you test how your current message might land with different kinds of listeners (a teenager, a skeptic, a longtime friend) and notice where it already shines and where a door could open wider.

  2. An immersive simulation

    A practice room where you try out how your words land, with real listeners' voices, and feel what lands and what doesn't.

  3. Your own curriculum

    Guided by inspiration and held inside clear boundaries and a rubric, so what you build is yours and still true to the model.

Feedback is built into the day

Listener feedback is not an afterthought. It is built into the workshop itself. People on the receiving end say, anonymously and through the tool, how it landed, where it landed, and where it didn't. Together we keep co-creating what success looks like, and we come to it from a spiritual lens.

Your materials

After the workshop, you build your own materials in an interactive web tool. You bring your inspirations, the ideas and phrases that feel true to you, and the tool turns them into talking guides and handouts in your own voice. The boundaries and the rubric stay fixed, so what you create is yours and still true to the model.

Everything you create is Creative Commons licensed and free for anyone to use.

Whose work this is

This work rests on the inspired word, not on any one person. Each trainer gives it voice in their own way.
