A VISUAL · THE WOBBLY WHEEL AND THE CUP

My Wheel and My Cup

The outside of a wheel bumps over everything. The middle stays still. You have a still middle too.

Cut me out! Push a pencil or a paper fastener through the heart and spin me. The outside goes around. The middle stays.

Draw one small kind thing in my cup.

Draw a time you felt wobbly.
