A FOLD-UP · THE CUP FOLDER

The Cup Folder

A paper fortune-teller of eight small cups. Say the middle first, then pick a number and see what your hand does.

Cut on the solid line. Fold on the dotted lines. Printed side down to fold — see page 2.

How to play, and the weather key

To fold it

  1. Cut out the square on the solid line.
  2. Put it printed side down. Fold each corner to the center dot and press the creases.
  3. Flip it over. Fold the four corners to the center again.
  4. Fold it in half one way and open it, then in half the other way and open it.
  5. Turn it over so the four labeled pockets show. Slide a thumb and finger of each hand into the pockets and pinch the points together.

How to play

  1. Say “Love is in the middle” out loud.
  2. Pick a move: BREATHE, PEEK, MIDDLE, or CUP, and open the folder that many times.
  3. Pick a number.
  4. Open the flap and read your cup.
  5. Do it, or do something close to it, this week.
