A CARD SORT · CUP OR SCOREBOARD?

Cup or Scoreboard?

Same act, different thought behind it. This is how to tell, and what to do when you weren’t even in the fight.

“Be still, and know that I am God.”

— Psalm 46:10, NIV

The Four Steps (for when you weren’t even in it)

1
NAME THE FEAR

What am I afraid of, exactly? Say it plainly. Naming it isn’t dwelling; it’s meeting.

2
SPOT THE STORY

What’s the picture in my head? It’s a picture, not the truth about anyone.

3
FIND THE MIDDLE

What’s true about both of them at once? Does it need a loser?

4
DO THE CUP

One small kind thing, specific, possible, something I’d do for either of them.

Four tests for a steady sentence

  • Present tense.
  • Plain words.
  • True of everyone at once.
  • Still true on the worst day.
