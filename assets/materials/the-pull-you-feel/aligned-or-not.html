A Visual · When You Really Like Someone

Aligned or Not?

Liking someone isn’t one feeling — it’s at least two, and they’re worth telling apart.

“There is but one I, or Us, but one divine Principle, or Mind, governing all existence; man and woman unchanged forever in their individual characters, even as numbers which never blend with each other, though they are governed by one Principle.”

— Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures, Mary Baker Eddy, pp. 588:9–19

Not Aligned Aligned
Not AlignedAligned
Has to be a secret to feel okayFeels fine out in the open
“I need them to text back RIGHT NOW”Can wait without falling apart
Trying to change who I am to get noticedStill feels like myself
Jealous or upset when they talk to anyone elseStill glad for them either way
Leaves me anxious, checking my phoneLeaves me calm, still able to focus

You get to like someone and still be fully you.

Notice

Think of someone you like right now (or made-up, if you’d rather) — which end of the dial is it closer to?
